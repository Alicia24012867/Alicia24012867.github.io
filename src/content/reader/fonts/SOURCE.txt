The "Alicia" signature SVG data in ../signatureStrokes.ts uses letter outlines
extracted from this font. The original curves are retained, with shared straight
cuts separating the regions belonging to successive pen strokes.

SVG pen trajectories animate directly with stroke-dasharray and stroke-dashoffset.
Static per-stroke clip paths constrain their ink to the original letter outlines.
Four thin linking strokes use adjacent letter outlines as their clip regions.
Connector segments are drawn faster; the two i dots follow the word's main strokes.
The complete outline is displayed while the finished signature holds and fades.
The signature does not load or display a font file at runtime.
