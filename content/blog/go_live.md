## Live

The website is live today, on the first day of being 20, after being refined day and night. It is a birthday gift to myself.

## What's here

A homepage, a few blog posts, and a notebook that has just started to grow.

- The homepage displays the basic information of myself.
- The blog holds complete articles across 2 sections - learning & life.
- The notes form a knowledge base, grouped by topic, with full-text search.

## The technical bits

The website is constructed using React, TypeScript and Vite. Formulas are rendered with KaTeX, diagrams with Mermaid,
and code with highlight.js. Markdown compilation, math and diagram rendering, full-text search, RSS — the content pipeline is watched over by 79 automated tests, and deployed automatically on every push. Article dates come from Git history, and every article gets its own share card.

## What isn't finished

The content is the biggest unfinished part: three posts(except this one), one note. The plan is to keep writing the notes, and to work through the list I made in [the eve-of post](./before_20th_birthday.md), one item at a time.

Besides, currently the website is a static one: every page is built ahead of time and served as plain files from GitHub Pages. Later, I plan to rent a server, add a backend, and turn it into an interactive, dynamic website.
